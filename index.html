Common Questions

Common Questions

How much does it cost to paint a house?

Pricing depends on the size of the home, surface condition, and the scope of work. The fastest way to get accurate pricing is to request a painting estimate for your specific project.

How long does interior painting take?

It depends on how many rooms are being painted and how much prep is needed. Small projects can move quickly, while larger interior jobs may take several days.

Do I need to move furniture before painting?

Clearing smaller items and valuables ahead of time helps the project move more smoothly. Larger furniture is usually easiest to discuss during the estimate.

How often should a Texas home exterior be painted?

That depends on sun exposure, weather, and the condition of the current finish. A fresh estimate is the best way to evaluate whether the exterior needs attention now.

Do you pressure wash before exterior painting?

Exterior surfaces often need to be cleaned before prep and paint begin. The right approach depends on the surface and the condition of the home.

How do I get a painting estimate?

Use the estimate form, choose the service you need, and send your contact details. That makes it easy to review the project and follow up with the next step.
